What you are comparing

A membership is for people who have taken the free class and want to keep training at 3630 S Cedar St, STE L. Group classes follow the weekly grid. The programs are Adult BJJ, Kids BJJ (ages 5+), the competition team, and private lessons.

Kids and adults can train in the same family. Start with the program that fits the person walking in: the kids page for ages 5 and up, or the adult page if you have never trained and want a beginner hour. The code of conduct, uniform patches, and mat rules are the same household to household. Read them on the homepage before the second visit.

There is no enrollment fee listed here because that number has not been confirmed either. If a registration fee, a uniform fee, or a testing fee exists, it belongs in the membership-term placeholder above, not in a surprise at the desk.
