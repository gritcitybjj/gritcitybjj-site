Home / Free Class

The first Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class at GRIT CITY BJJ is free. That is true for adults and for kids age 5 and up. You do not need experience, and you do not need to buy a uniform before you have seen the room. The academy is in Cedar Plaza at 3630 S Cedar St, STE L, Tacoma, WA 98409, and has been Teaching Tacoma Since 2011.

What to wear

Wear a T-shirt and athletic pants or shorts you can move in. Bring a water bottle. Take off jewelry, and trim your fingernails and toenails so you do not scratch a partner. Come barefoot onto the mat. Shoes stop at the edge. If you already own a clean gi with no other school's patches, you can wear it, but it is not required for this visit. Academy patch rules apply after you join. [PLACEHOLDER: gi color and where to buy a gi].

Kids should wear the same kind of athletic clothes. A parent or guardian stays for the first visit. Spectators sit in the designated area, keep phones quiet, and leave the coaching to the coaches.

What to expect

Arrive a few minutes early so you can meet the coach and see where to stand. Class starts with a warm-up, moves into a technique you can drill slowly, and may include controlled partner work if you are comfortable. You can sit out a round. Say so. Nobody is signed up for a tournament because they took a free class. Competition stays optional.

Adults who have never trained should read the adult BJJ page so the hour makes sense before you arrive. Parents should read the kids page for how confidence, discipline, and anti-bullying show up in a normal month. The schedule lists kids, adult, no-gi, kickboxing, and open mat. Exact times that are still unconfirmed are marked on that page. Tell us which evenings you can make and we will point you at a real class.

If you are late, wait off the mat until a coach invites you on. Bow when you step on and when you step off. The rest of the mat rules are in the code of conduct.

Parking and the front door

The address is 3630 S Cedar St, STE L, Tacoma, WA 98409, inside Cedar Plaza. Use the map if you have not been to the plaza before: open directions. Which lot to use, and which door is closest to the academy, is [PLACEHOLDER: Cedar Plaza parking and entrance details]. If you get turned around, call 253-254-6538 from the parking lot.

After you send the form

The form on this page goes to the same inbox as the homepage form. You should get a message connected to gritcitybjj@gmail.com. Check spam if it is not in the main inbox within a day. A coach or the front desk will confirm a class time. If you would rather talk, call 253-254-6538.

The free class is not a bill. Membership prices, the family discount, and the military discount are on the pricing page. Where a number is not confirmed, it is marked there instead of invented here. You can decide after class. There is no speech you have to sit through in order to leave.

Book the free class

Send your name, a way to reach you, and who the class is for. A parent books for a child. An adult books for themselves. If a friend is coming with you, say so in the message.
