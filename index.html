Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu FAQs

1. What can BJJ and your school do for me?
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu builds real confidence, functional strength, and mental discipline. Training consistently improves your conditioning, mobility, focus, stress management, and overall mindset. Students also gain a powerful sense of community. You are not just joining a gym. You are joining a culture built around respect, accountability, and personal growth. Whether your goal is self-defense, fitness, competition, or a life change, BJJ gives you measurable progress month after month.

2. Do I need to be in shape to start?
No. BJJ gets you in shape. Classes scale to your level. Beginners start slow and learn positioning, drills, and controlled technique. You build cardio through training. Everyone starts at zero.

3. Am I too old?
No. We have students in their teens, 20s, 30s, 40s, 50s, and beyond. BJJ is a lifelong martial art that adapts to your body and your pace. Your age is not a barrier. Your consistency is.

4. Will I be expected to compete?
Competition is optional. You will never be pushed to compete unless you want to. We offer competition training for those who choose that path, but regular classes are designed for all skill levels and all goals, including lifestyle, fitness, knowledge, and self-defense.

5. Do I need prior martial arts experience?
None. Most students start with zero experience. The curriculum is structured for beginners first, then builds layer by layer. You learn fundamentals at your own pace. Read the adult BJJ page for how a first class works.

6. Do you offer beginner-friendly classes?
Yes. Classes teach the core positions, movements, transitions, and submissions in a controlled format. Instructors guide every step so beginners are not left behind.

7. What programs do you offer?
Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu for ages 5 and up, a competition team, and private lessons. Kids details are on the kids page. Adult classes and the competition team are on the adult page. Private lesson rates are on the pricing page.

8. Will BJJ help me lose weight?
Rolling, drilling, and class structure burn calories and build lean muscle. Many students notice changes in weight, energy, and confidence in the first weeks. Results depend on consistency, sleep, and how you eat.

9. What does a typical class look like?
Classes usually follow this structure:
Warm-up: mobility, movement patterns, light conditioning.
Technique: step-by-step instruction, drilling, and learning positions.
Live training: situational training, controlled rolls, and optional sparring.
Each class builds on the previous one.

10. Do you offer family or multi-student discounts?
Yes. We support families training together and offer reduced pricing for households with more than one member training. The amount is [PLACEHOLDER: family discount] and is listed on the pricing page.

11. How much does membership cost?
Options for adults, kids, and private lessons are on the pricing page. Rates that are still being confirmed are marked there. Call 253-254-6538 and we will help you pick a plan. The first class is free.

12. What age can kids start?
Kids classes start at age 5. The program builds confidence, discipline, coordination, and anti-bullying habits, along with real martial arts skills in a supervised room. Details are on the kids BJJ page.

13. How long does it take to earn a black belt?
BJJ is earned, not given. On average it takes 8 to 12 years, depending on consistency, attitude, effort, and dedication. Every belt represents real skill and real work.

14. How do I get started?
Book your free class, show up in comfortable athletic clothing, and be ready to learn. You can also call 253-254-6538. The form is on this page and on the free class page.
